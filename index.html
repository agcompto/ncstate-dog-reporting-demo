Individual gift reporting

Online and offline reports remain distinct, as in the historical unit dashboards. Online gifts include all five explicit UTM parameters, illustrative unique tracking-link attribution, and whether an advocate is associated with the gift (including advocate name and ID where present). GiveCampus field availability and attribution rules require confirmation. Fund attribution is based on the fund, not the page the donor visited.

About this reporting concept

This illustrates the short-term operational reporting units could use during the VIP window and Day of Giving. It is not a CRM or constituent analytics dashboard. Comprehensive reporting would take place later in Lobo.

In a production solution, access would be managed by NC State, preferably through university SSO and unit assignments. This public sample includes no login, API, database, tracking, or actual donor records. Refunds and adjustments would follow GiveCampus reporting conventions.
